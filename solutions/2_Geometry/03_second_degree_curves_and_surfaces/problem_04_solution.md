# Solution 4 - Parabola

## Problem statement

For

$$
y^2=12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find the points with $x=3$ and describe a sketch.

## Lettered quantities

- Let $p>0$ denote the focal parameter in $y^2=4px$.

## Step 1 - Find

**Parabola parameter.** In $y^2=4px$, the coefficient $4p$ determines $p$, while the vertex is $(0,0)$. Compare:
 the focal parameter

Compare the coefficients of $x$:

$$
4p=12,
$$

so

$$
p=\frac{12}{4}=3.
$$

## Step 2 - Read

**Parabola features reminder.** For $y^2=4px$, the axis is $y=0$, the focus is $(p,0)$, and the right-hand opening is because $p>0$. State:
 the geometric features

The vertex is $(0,0)$, the axis of symmetry is $y=0$, and the parabola opens in the positive $x$ direction. The focus is $(3,0)$, and the directrix is $x=-3$.

## Step 3 - Evaluate

**Point substitution.** A point is on $y^2=12x$ if its coordinates satisfy the equation. Substitute $x=3$:
 the points with $x=3$

Substitute $x=3$:

$$
y^2=12(3)=36.
$$

Taking both square roots gives

$$
y=\pm6.
$$

The requested points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

## Verification

For $(3,6)$,

$$
6^2=36=12(3).
$$

For $(3,-6)$,

$$
(-6)^2=36=12(3).
$$

Both points therefore lie on the curve, and their symmetric heights confirm the $x$-axis as the symmetry axis.

## Geometric interpretation

The parabola has its narrow endpoint at the origin and widens as $x$ increases. The two points $(3,\pm6)$ show the separation of the branches at $x=3$.

## Final answer

The vertex is $(0,0)$, the axis is $y=0$, and the parabola opens right. Its focus is $(3,0)$ and directrix is $x=-3$. For $x=3$, the points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.
