# Solution 4 — Parabola

## Problem Statement

For

$$
y^2=12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points for $x=3$ and describe a sketch.

## Objective

Interpret the standard form of a horizontal parabola and use a coordinate slice to find points.

## Concepts and Formulas

The standard equation $y^2=4px$ has vertex $(0,0)$, axis the $x$-axis, and opens right when $p>0$. Its focus is $(p,0)$ and directrix is $x=-p$.

## Step-by-Step Solution

Comparing $y^2=12x$ and $y^2=4px$ gives $4p=12$, hence $p=3$. The vertex is $(0,0)$, the axis of symmetry is $y=0$, and the opening direction is positive $x$. The focus is $(3,0)$ and the directrix is $x=-3$.

At $x=3$:

$$
y^2=12(3)=36,
\qquad
y=\pm6.
$$

The two points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$. Sketch a right-opening curve with its vertex at the origin, symmetric about the $x$-axis, passing through these two points.

## Verification

For each of $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$, the left side is $36$ and the right side is $12(3)=36$.

## Final Answer

The vertex is $(0,0)$, the axis is the $x$-axis, and the parabola opens right. For $x=3$, its points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

## Key Takeaways

- Comparing with $y^2=4px$ identifies the orientation and focal parameter.
- A fixed $x$-value generally produces two symmetric $y$-values.
