# Problem 7. A Trigonometric Function

## Problem

On $[0,2\pi]$, find monotonicity and extrema of $f(x)=\sin x+\cos x$, including endpoints.

## Solution

Let $f(x)=\sin x+\cos x$.

### Critical points

$$
f'(x)=\cos x-\sin x.
$$

Solving $\cos x=\sin x$ gives

$$
x=\pi/4,\qquad x=5\pi/4.
$$

### Sign and monotonicity

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(0,\pi/4)\cup(5\pi/4,2\pi):&&f'(x)>0,\\
&(\pi/4,5\pi/4):&&f'(x)<0.
\end{aligned}
$$

### Endpoint and critical values

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(0)&=1,\\
f(\pi/4)&=\sqrt2,\\
f(5\pi/4)&=-\sqrt2,\\
f(2\pi)&=1.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first critical point is an absolute maximum and the second is an absolute minimum. The endpoints are equal but give neither absolute extreme.

## Final Answer

Increasing on $(0,\pi/4)\cup(5\pi/4,2\pi)$ and decreasing on $(\pi/4,5\pi/4)$; absolute maximum $(\pi/4,\sqrt2)$; absolute minimum $(5\pi/4,-\sqrt2)$.
